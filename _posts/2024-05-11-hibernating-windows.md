I can remember seeing hibernate as an option when shutting down a laptop when I was much younger. I wondered where the option is now, and I accidentally met it last night.

I have dual booted my laptop. It currently has _Windows_ and _Linux Mint_ installed. I wanted to switch to _Linux Mint_ without having to close my already opened apps. So I opened `powershell`, typed `shutdown /h` in hopes of seeing the help for `shutdown.exe`. Instead of getting the help message for `shutdown`, my laptop hibernated!

Now I know how to hibernate my laptop _my way_.

> By the way, windows has an article that shows how to enable the hibernate option: <https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/shut-down-sleep-or-hibernate-your-pc-2941d165-7d0a-a5e8-c5ad-8c972e8e6eff>
